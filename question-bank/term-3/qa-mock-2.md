**Q1.** Answer in Spanish: **¿Te gusta la química?** *(say no, you hate it because it's difficult)*

<details><summary>▶ Answer & explanation</summary>

**The question means:** "Do you like chemistry?" — say no, you hate it because it's difficult.
**✅ Model answer:**
- 🇪🇸 **No, odio la química porque es difícil.**
- 🇬🇧 *No, I hate chemistry because it's difficult.*
</details>

**Q15.** Write 3–4 sentences in Spanish describing your school day: a subject you have, what time, your opinion of it, and one place in your school.

<details><summary>▶ Answer & explanation</summary>

**The question means:** Write a short Term‑3 paragraph about your school day.
**✅ Model answer:**
- 🇪🇸 **Los lunes tengo historia a las nueve. Me gusta porque es interesante. En mi instituto hay una biblioteca grande y un patio. ¡Mi día favorito es el viernes!**

</details>
