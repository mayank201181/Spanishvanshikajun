**Q1.** **¿De dónde eres?** is asking…
- A) Where do you live?
- B) Where are you from?
- C) What's your name?
- D) How old are you?

<details><summary>▶ Answer & explanation</summary>

**The question means:** What is *¿De dónde eres?* asking?
**✅ Correct: B) Where are you from?**
- 🇬🇧 *Where are you from?*
- 🇪🇸 You answer **Soy de…** (I am from…)
</details>

---

**Q2.** How do you say **I am from England**?
- A) Vivo en Inglaterra
- B) Soy de Inglaterra
- C) Soy Inglaterra
- D) Hablo inglés

<details><summary>▶ Answer & explanation</summary>

**The question means:** Choose the correct Spanish for "I am from England".
**✅ Correct: B) Soy de Inglaterra**
- 🇬🇧 *I am from England*
- 🇪🇸 **Soy de Inglaterra** — *soy de* = I am from. (*Vivo en* = I live in.)
</details>
