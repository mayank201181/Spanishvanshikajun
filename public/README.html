🇪🇸 Spanish Year 7 — Revision Guide & Question Bank

A complete, illustrated revision pack built from the Spanish 7A Knowledge Organiser (SentenceBuilders) notebook. It is designed so you can learn and memorise the vocabulary first, then test yourself with realistic mock exams.

How the mock tests work: Read each question and write/say your answer first. Then click the ▶ "Answer & explanation" arrow underneath. It tells you, in plain English, what the question was asking, and gives you the correct answer in English and in Spanish. No peeking until you've tried! 😊

🗓️ A suggested 6‑day revision plan

Day Do this
Day 1 Read the Term 1 guide, cover the English column and test yourself on the words.
Day 2 Read the Term 2 guide, then do Term 1 → MCQ Mock 1.
Day 3 Read the Term 3 guide, then do Term 2 → MCQ Mock 1.
Day 4 Do Term 3 → MCQ Mock 1 and one Q&A mock of your choice.
Day 5 Re‑read the full‑year cheat‑sheet, redo any mock where you scored under 20/25.
Day 6 Do Full‑Year → MCQ Mock 1 + Q&A Mock 1 as a dress rehearsal. ⭐
