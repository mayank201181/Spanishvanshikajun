🟥 Term 3 — Question & Answer Mock Test 2

15 written questions. Write a full sentence first, then open the arrow.

Q1. Answer in Spanish: ¿Te gusta la química? (say no, you hate it because it's difficult)

▶ Answer & explanation

The question means: "Do you like chemistry?" — say no, you hate it because it's difficult. ✅ Model answer:

  • 🇪🇸 No, odio la química porque es difícil.
  • 🇬🇧 No, I hate chemistry because it's difficult.

Q2. Translate into Spanish: I prefer geography because it's useful.

▶ Answer & explanation

The question means: Put the opinion sentence into Spanish. ✅ Model answer:

  • 🇪🇸 Prefiero la geografía porque es útil.
  • 🇬🇧 I prefer geography because it's useful.

Q3. Translate into English: Me gustan las ciencias pero no me gusta la física.

▶ Answer & explanation

The question means: Put the Spanish into English. ✅ Model answer:

  • 🇬🇧 I like science but I don't like physics.
  • 🇪🇸 Me gustan = I like (plural), no me gusta = I don't like.

Q4. Answer in Spanish: ¿Cuántas asignaturas estudias? (say you study nine subjects)

▶ Answer & explanation

The question means: "How many subjects do you study?" — say nine. ✅ Model answer:

  • 🇪🇸 Estudio nueve asignaturas.
  • 🇬🇧 I study nine subjects.

Q5. Translate into Spanish: My music teacher is fun but a bit strict. (male teacher)

▶ Answer & explanation

The question means: Put the sentence into Spanish (male teacher). ✅ Model answer:

  • 🇪🇸 Mi profesor de música es divertido pero un poco estricto.
  • 🇬🇧 My music teacher is fun but a bit strict.

Q6. Translate into English: Mi profesora de arte se llama señora López y es muy creativa.

▶ Answer & explanation

The question means: Put the Spanish into English. ✅ Model answer:

  • 🇬🇧 My art teacher is called Mrs López and she is very creative.
  • 🇪🇸 se llama = is called, creativa = creative.

Q7. Answer in Spanish: ¿Hay un comedor en tu instituto? (say yes, and there's also a big library)

▶ Answer & explanation

The question means: "Is there a canteen in your school?" — say yes, plus a big library. ✅ Model answer:

  • 🇪🇸 Sí, hay un comedor y también hay una biblioteca grande.
  • 🇬🇧 Yes, there is a canteen and there is also a big library. (también = also.)

Q8. Translate into Spanish: In my school there are lots of classrooms and a laboratory.

▶ Answer & explanation

The question means: Put the sentence into Spanish. ✅ Model answer:

  • 🇪🇸 En mi instituto hay muchas aulas y un laboratorio.
  • 🇬🇧 In my school there are lots of classrooms and a laboratory. (muchas aulas = lots of classrooms.)

Q9. Write in Spanish: It's twenty past six.

▶ Answer & explanation

The question means: Write the time "twenty past six". ✅ Model answer:

  • 🇪🇸 Son las seis y veinte.
  • 🇬🇧 It's twenty past six. (Use y for "past" + the minutes.)

Q10. Write in Spanish: It's exactly four o'clock.

▶ Answer & explanation

The question means: Write "It's exactly 4 o'clock". ✅ Model answer:

  • 🇪🇸 Son las cuatro en punto.
  • 🇬🇧 It's exactly four o'clock. (en punto = exactly / on the dot.)

Q11. Translate into Spanish: On Wednesdays I have PE first lesson and it's fun.

▶ Answer & explanation

The question means: Put the timetable sentence into Spanish. ✅ Model answer:

  • 🇪🇸 Los miércoles tengo educación física a primera hora y es divertido.
  • 🇬🇧 On Wednesdays I have PE first lesson and it's fun.

Q12. Translate into English: A las once tengo el recreo y como con mis amigos.

▶ Answer & explanation

The question means: Put the Spanish into English. ✅ Model answer:

  • 🇬🇧 At eleven o'clock I have break and I eat with my friends.
  • 🇪🇸 el recreo = break, como = I eat, mis amigos = my friends.

Q13. Answer in Spanish: ¿Cuál es tu día favorito y por qué? (say Friday, because you have art and music)

▶ Answer & explanation

The question means: "What's your favourite day and why?" — Friday, art and music. ✅ Model answer:

  • 🇪🇸 Mi día favorito es el viernes porque tengo arte y música.
  • 🇬🇧 My favourite day is Friday because I have art and music.

Q14. Translate into Spanish: I love ICT because it's useful and modern, but I hate RE because it's boring.

▶ Answer & explanation

The question means: Put the longer opinion sentence into Spanish. ✅ Model answer:

  • 🇪🇸 Me encanta la informática porque es útil y moderna, pero odio la religión porque es aburrida.
  • 🇬🇧 I love ICT because it's useful and modern, but I hate RE because it's boring.

Q15. Write 3–4 sentences in Spanish describing your school day: a subject you have, what time, your opinion of it, and one place in your school.

▶ Answer & explanation

The question means: Write a short Term‑3 paragraph about your school day. ✅ Model answer:

  • 🇪🇸 Los lunes tengo historia a las nueve. Me gusta porque es interesante. En mi instituto hay una biblioteca grande y un patio. ¡Mi día favorito es el viernes!
  • 🇬🇧 On Mondays I have history at nine. I like it because it's interesting. In my school there is a big library and a playground. My favourite day is Friday!
