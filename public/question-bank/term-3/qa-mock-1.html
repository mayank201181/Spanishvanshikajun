🟥 Term 3 — Question & Answer Mock Test 1

15 written questions on subjects & opinions, teachers, your school, timetable and the time. Write a full sentence first, then open the arrow.

Q1. Answer in Spanish: ¿Cuál es tu asignatura favorita? (say it's art)

▶ Answer & explanation

The question means: "What is your favourite subject?" — say art. ✅ Model answer:

  • 🇪🇸 Mi asignatura favorita es el arte.
  • 🇬🇧 My favourite subject is art.

Q2. Translate into Spanish: I like history because it's interesting.

▶ Answer & explanation

The question means: Put the opinion sentence into Spanish. ✅ Model answer:

  • 🇪🇸 Me gusta la historia porque es interesante.
  • 🇬🇧 I like history because it's interesting.

Q3. Translate into Spanish: I love science because it's fun. (remember: science = plural)

▶ Answer & explanation

The question means: Put the sentence into Spanish — careful with gustar/encantar and a plural subject. ✅ Model answer:

  • 🇪🇸 Me encantan las ciencias porque son divertidas.
  • 🇬🇧 I love science because it's fun. (Plural subject → encantan and son.)

Q4. Translate into English: Odio las matemáticas porque son difíciles y aburridas.

▶ Answer & explanation

The question means: Put the Spanish into English. ✅ Model answer:

  • 🇬🇧 I hate maths because it's difficult and boring.
  • 🇪🇸 Odio = I hate, difíciles = difficult, aburridas = boring.

Q5. Answer in Spanish: ¿Qué estudias? (say you study English, maths and geography)

▶ Answer & explanation

The question means: "What do you study?" — list three subjects. ✅ Model answer:

  • 🇪🇸 Estudio inglés, matemáticas y geografía.
  • 🇬🇧 I study English, maths and geography.

Q6. Translate into Spanish: My English teacher is nice and patient. (female teacher)

▶ Answer & explanation

The question means: Put the sentence into Spanish (female teacher). ✅ Model answer:

  • 🇪🇸 Mi profesora de inglés es simpática y paciente.
  • 🇬🇧 My English teacher is nice and patient. (profesora = female; simpática ‑a.)

Q7. Translate into English: Mi profesor de matemáticas es muy estricto pero justo.

▶ Answer & explanation

The question means: Put the Spanish into English. ✅ Model answer:

  • 🇬🇧 My maths teacher is very strict but fair.
  • 🇪🇸 estricto = strict, justo = fair.

Q8. Answer in Spanish: ¿Qué hay en tu instituto? (say there is a library, a gym and a canteen)

▶ Answer & explanation

The question means: "What is there in your school?" — name three places. ✅ Model answer:

  • 🇪🇸 En mi instituto hay una biblioteca, un gimnasio y un comedor.
  • 🇬🇧 In my school there is a library, a gym and a canteen.

Q9. Translate into Spanish: In my school there is a football pitch but there isn't a swimming pool.

▶ Answer & explanation

The question means: Put the sentence into Spanish. ✅ Model answer:

  • 🇪🇸 En mi instituto hay un campo de fútbol pero no hay piscina.
  • 🇬🇧 In my school there is a football pitch but there isn't a swimming pool.

Q10. Answer in Spanish: ¿Qué hora es? (say it's half past three)

▶ Answer & explanation

The question means: "What time is it?" — say half past three. ✅ Model answer:

  • 🇪🇸 Son las tres y media.
  • 🇬🇧 It's half past three.

Q11. Write in Spanish: It's one o'clock.

▶ Answer & explanation

The question means: Write "It's 1 o'clock" — watch the special form! ✅ Model answer:

  • 🇪🇸 Es la una.
  • 🇬🇧 It's one o'clock. (Only 1 o'clock uses Es la.)

Q12. Write in Spanish: It's quarter to nine.

▶ Answer & explanation

The question means: Write the time "quarter to nine". ✅ Model answer:

  • 🇪🇸 Son las nueve menos cuarto.
  • 🇬🇧 It's quarter to nine. (menos cuarto = quarter to.)

Q13. Translate into Spanish: On Mondays I have music at ten o'clock.

▶ Answer & explanation

The question means: Put the timetable sentence into Spanish. ✅ Model answer:

  • 🇪🇸 Los lunes tengo música a las diez.
  • 🇬🇧 On Mondays I have music at ten o'clock. (Los lunes = on Mondays, a las diez = at ten.)

Q14. Translate into English: Mi día favorito es el viernes porque tengo arte y educación física.

▶ Answer & explanation

The question means: Put the Spanish into English. ✅ Model answer:

  • 🇬🇧 My favourite day is Friday because I have art and PE.
  • 🇪🇸 día favorito = favourite day, educación física = PE.

Q15. Write 3 sentences in Spanish: a subject you love (with a reason), a subject you hate (with a reason), and what your favourite teacher is like.

▶ Answer & explanation

The question means: Write a short Term‑3 opinion paragraph. ✅ Model answer:

  • 🇪🇸 Me encanta el arte porque es divertido y relajante. Odio las matemáticas porque son difíciles. Mi profesora de inglés es simpática y muy paciente.
  • 🇬🇧 I love art because it's fun and relaxing. I hate maths because it's difficult. My English teacher is nice and very patient.
  • ✔️ Use Me encanta… / Odio… porque es/son… / Mi profesor(a) … es …
