Q1. ¿Cómo estás? is asking…

  • A) What's your name?
  • B) How are you?
  • C) How old are you?
  • D) Where are you from?
▶ Answer & explanation

The question means: What is ¿Cómo estás? asking? ✅ Correct: B) How are you?

  • 🇬🇧 How are you?
  • 🇪🇸 You answer Estoy bien / mal…

Q2. Estoy fenomenal means…

  • A) I am terrible
  • B) I am great
  • C) I am bored
  • D) I am ill
▶ Answer & explanation

The question means: Translate Estoy fenomenal. ✅ Correct: B) I am great

  • 🇬🇧 I am great / fantastic
  • 🇪🇸 fenomenal = great. (fatal = terrible, enfermo = ill)

Q3. Which number is veintiocho?

  • A) 18
  • B) 28
  • C) 38
  • D) 80
▶ Answer & explanation

The question means: What number is veintiocho? ✅ Correct: B) 28

  • 🇬🇧 twenty‑eight
  • 🇪🇸 veinte 20 + ocho 8 = 28.

  • 🇪🇸 jueves (martes = Tuesday, sábado = Saturday, domingo = Sunday)

Q18. Choose the right word: Mi hermana ___ trece años.

Q23. How do you say My favourite subject is science?

  • B) Mi asignatura favorita son las ciencias
  • C) Me gusta las ciencias favorita

▶ Answer & explanation

The question means: Choose the correct sentence (science = plural). ✅ Correct: B) Mi asignatura favorita son las ciencias

  • 🇬🇧 My favourite subject is science
  • 🇪🇸 las ciencias is plural → use son (are), not es.

Q24. Soy de Inglaterra, soy inglés y hablo inglés. Which is NOT true?

  • A) He is from England
  • B) He is English
  • C) He speaks English
  • D) He lives in Spain
▶ Answer & explanation

The question means: Read and choose the statement that is NOT said. ✅ Correct: D) He lives in Spain

  • 🇬🇧 The sentence says he's from England, is English, and speaks English — it does not mention living in Spain.
  • 🇪🇸 Soy de… / soy inglés / hablo inglés.

Q25. Which sentence correctly uses estar and ser?

  • A) Soy cansada y estoy simpática
  • B) Estoy cansada y soy simpática
  • C) Tengo cansada y soy simpática
  • D) Estoy cansada y estoy española
▶ Answer & explanation

The question means: Choose the sentence that uses estar (feelings) and ser (personality) correctly. ✅ Correct: B) Estoy cansada y soy simpática

  • 🇬🇧 I am tired and I am nice
  • 🇪🇸 Estoy for the feeling (cansada/tired), soy for the personality (simpática/nice).
