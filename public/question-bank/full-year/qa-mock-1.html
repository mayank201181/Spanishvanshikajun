🟩 Full Year — Question & Answer Mock Test 1 (cumulative)

15 written questions mixing all three terms. Write a full sentence first, then open the arrow.

Q1. Answer in Spanish: ¿Cómo te llamas y cuántos años tienes? (say Ana, 12)

▶ Answer & explanation

The question means: "What's your name and how old are you?" — Ana, 12. ✅ Model answer:

  • 🇪🇸 Me llamo Ana y tengo doce años.
  • 🇬🇧 My name is Ana and I am 12 years old.

Q2. Translate into Spanish: I am happy because today is Friday. (girl)

▶ Answer & explanation

The question means: Put the feelings sentence into Spanish. ✅ Model answer:

  • 🇪🇸 Estoy contenta porque hoy es viernes.
  • 🇬🇧 I am happy because today is Friday. (Feeling → estoy.)

Q3. Answer in Spanish: ¿Cuándo es tu cumpleaños? (say the 22nd of November)

▶ Answer & explanation

The question means: "When is your birthday?" — 22nd of November. ✅ Model answer:

  • 🇪🇸 Mi cumpleaños es el veintidós de noviembre.
  • 🇬🇧 My birthday is the 22nd of November.

Q4. Translate into Spanish: In my bag I have a blue pencil case and a red ruler.

▶ Answer & explanation

The question means: Put the sentence into Spanish (watch the colour endings). ✅ Model answer:

  • 🇪🇸 En mi mochila tengo un estuche azul y una regla roja.
  • 🇬🇧 In my bag I have a blue pencil case and a red ruler.

Q5. Translate into English: Tengo el pelo corto y rizado y soy bastante alta.

▶ Answer & explanation

The question means: Put the Spanish into English. ✅ Model answer:

  • 🇬🇧 I have short, curly hair and I am quite tall.
  • 🇪🇸 corto = short, rizado = curly, bastante = quite, alta = tall.

▶ Answer & explanation

Q15. Write 4 sentences in Spanish introducing yourself: your name & age, how you feel today, where you're from / live, and your favourite subject.

▶ Answer & explanation

The question means: Write a short paragraph using language from all three terms. ✅ Model answer:

  • 🇪🇸 Me llamo Sofía y tengo doce años. Hoy estoy muy contenta. Soy inglesa y vivo en un pueblo en la costa. Mi asignatura favorita es el arte porque es divertido.
  • 🇬🇧 My name is Sofía and I am 12 years old. Today I am very happy. I am English and I live in a town on the coast. My favourite subject is art because it's fun.
  • ✔️ This shows off Me llamo / Tengo … años / Estoy … / Soy … / Vivo en … / Mi asignatura favorita es … — a great full‑year answer!
