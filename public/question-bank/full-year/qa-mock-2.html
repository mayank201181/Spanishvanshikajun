🟩 Full Year — Question & Answer Mock Test 2 (cumulative)

15 written questions mixing all three terms. This is your final dress rehearsal! Write a full sentence first, then open the arrow.

Q1. Answer in Spanish: ¿Cómo estás hoy y por qué? (say you're nervous because you have an exam — girl)

▶ Answer & explanation

The question means: "How are you today and why?" — nervous, because of an exam. ✅ Model answer:

  • 🇪🇸 Estoy nerviosa porque tengo un examen.
  • 🇬🇧 I am nervous because I have an exam.

Q2. Translate into Spanish: I am 12 years old and my birthday is the 3rd of April.

▶ Answer & explanation

The question means: Put the sentence into Spanish. ✅ Model answer:

  • 🇪🇸 Tengo doce años y mi cumpleaños es el tres de abril.
  • 🇬🇧 I am 12 years old and my birthday is the 3rd of April.

Q3. Translate into English: No tengo hermanos; soy hija única.

▶ Answer & explanation

The question means: Put the Spanish into English. ✅ Model answer:

  • 🇬🇧 I don't have brothers or sisters; I am an only child.
  • 🇪🇸 hermanos = siblings, hija única = only child (girl).

Q4. Translate into Spanish: My mum is tall and funny, and my dad is nice but a bit serious.

▶ Answer & explanation

The question means: Put the description into Spanish. ✅ Model answer:

  • 🇪🇸 Mi madre es alta y graciosa, y mi padre es simpático pero un poco serio.
  • 🇬🇧 My mum is tall and funny, and my dad is nice but a bit serious.

Q5. Look at the picture and write a full sentence in Spanish: "I have a …"

▶ Answer & explanation

The question means: Write "I have a rabbit" (the animal in the photo). ✅ Model answer:

  • 🇪🇸 Tengo un conejo. (even better: Tengo un conejo blanco. Se llama Copo.)
  • 🇬🇧 I have a rabbit. (a white rabbit. It's called Copo.)

Q6. Answer in Spanish: ¿De dónde eres y dónde vives? (say from Wales, living in a city in England)

▶ Answer & explanation

The question means: "Where are you from and where do you live?" — from Wales, live in a city in England. ✅ Model answer:

  • 🇪🇸 Soy de Gales y vivo en una ciudad en Inglaterra.
  • 🇬🇧 I am from Wales and I live in a city in England.

▶ Answer & explanation

The question means: Write the time "quarter to eleven". ✅ Model answer:

  • 🇪🇸 Son las once menos cuarto.
  • 🇬🇧 It's quarter to eleven. (menos cuarto = quarter to.)

Q15. Write 4–5 sentences in Spanish — a full self‑introduction for the exam: name & age, feeling, where you're from / live, nationality & a language, and a subject you love with a reason.

▶ Answer & explanation

The question means: Write a longer paragraph that shows off language from every term. ✅ Model answer:

  • 🇪🇸 ¡Hola! Me llamo Lucía y tengo doce años. Hoy estoy muy contenta. Soy de Inglaterra y vivo en un pueblo en la costa. Soy inglesa y hablo inglés y un poco de español. Mi asignatura favorita es el arte porque es divertido y relajante.
  • 🇬🇧 Hi! My name is Lucía and I am 12 years old. Today I am very happy. I am from England and I live in a town on the coast. I am English and I speak English and a little Spanish. My favourite subject is art because it's fun and relaxing.
  • ✔️ A top answer connects ideas with y (and), pero (but) and porque (because), and uses Me llamo / Tengo / Estoy / Soy / Vivo / Hablo / Me gusta. ¡Muy bien hecho! 🌟
