Q1. Estoy muy nerviosa means…

  • A) I am very calm
  • B) I am very nervous
  • C) I am very happy
  • D) I am very tired
▶ Answer & explanation

The question means: Translate Estoy muy nerviosa. ✅ Correct: B) I am very nervous

  • 🇬🇧 I am very nervous (girl)
  • 🇪🇸 nervioso/a = nervous. (Feelings use estoy.)

▶ Answer & explanation
