🟨 Term 2 — MCQ Mock Test 2

25 multiple‑choice questions. Answer first, then open the arrow.

Q1. How do you say I am from Scotland?

  • A) Vivo en Escocia
  • B) Soy de Escocia
  • C) Hablo escocés
  • D) Soy Escocia
▶ Answer & explanation

The question means: Choose the Spanish for "I am from Scotland". ✅ Correct: B) Soy de Escocia

  • 🇬🇧 I am from Scotland
  • 🇪🇸 Soy de Escocia.

Q2. What does Vivo en las montañas mean?

  • A) I live on the coast
  • B) I live in the mountains
  • C) I live in a city
  • D) I live in the countryside
▶ Answer & explanation

The question means: Translate Vivo en las montañas. ✅ Correct: B) I live in the mountains

  • 🇬🇧 I live in the mountains
  • 🇪🇸 las montañas = the mountains.

Q3. Which means a big city?

▶ Answer & explanation

The question means: Which phrase means "a big city"? ✅ Correct: B) una ciudad grande

  • 🇬🇧 a big city

Q7. ¿Dónde vives? — best answer:

Q10. Which is the language, not the country?

Q21. Soy escocesa pero vivo en Londres. What is true?

  • A) She is Scottish and lives in Scotland
  • B) She is Scottish but lives in London
  • C) She is English and lives in London
  • D) She is from London
▶ Answer & explanation

The question means: Read and choose the true statement. ✅ Correct: B) She is Scottish but lives in London

  • 🇬🇧 I am Scottish but I live in London
  • 🇪🇸 escocesa = Scottish (girl), pero = but, vivo en = I live in.

Q22. Which is the odd one out (not a country)?

  • A) Francia
  • B) Irlanda
  • C) español
  • D) Alemania
▶ Answer & explanation

The question means: Three are countries; one is not. ✅ Correct: C) español

  • 🇬🇧 español = Spanish (a nationality/language), not a country. (Country = España.)
  • 🇪🇸 Francia, Irlanda, Alemania are countries.

Q24. Mi padre es alemán y mi madre es española. What is true?

  • A) Both parents are Spanish
  • B) Dad is German, Mum is Spanish
  • C) Dad is Spanish, Mum is German
  • D) Both parents are German
▶ Answer & explanation

The question means: Read the sentence and choose the true statement. ✅ Correct: B) Dad is German, Mum is Spanish

  • 🇬🇧 My dad is German and my mum is Spanish
  • 🇪🇸 padre = father (alemán = German), madre = mother (española = Spanish).

Q25. Put in order — which is a correct full sentence?

  • A) Soy de Inglaterra y vivo en un pueblo en la costa.
  • B) Inglaterra de soy costa vivo pueblo.
  • C) Vivo soy de en Inglaterra.
  • D) De Inglaterra costa vivo soy.
▶ Answer & explanation

The question means: Choose the sentence with correct word order. ✅ Correct: A) Soy de Inglaterra y vivo en un pueblo en la costa.

  • 🇬🇧 I am from England and I live in a town on the coast.
  • 🇪🇸 The verb phrases Soy de… and vivo en… must stay together and in order.
