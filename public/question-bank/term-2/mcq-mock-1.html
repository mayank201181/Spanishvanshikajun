🟨 Term 2 — MCQ Mock Test 1

25 multiple‑choice questions on where you're from, where you live, nationalities and languages. Answer first, then open the arrow.

Q1. ¿De dónde eres? is asking…

  • A) Where do you live?
  • B) Where are you from?
  • C) What's your name?
  • D) How old are you?
▶ Answer & explanation

The question means: What is ¿De dónde eres? asking? ✅ Correct: B) Where are you from?

  • 🇬🇧 Where are you from?
  • 🇪🇸 You answer Soy de… (I am from…)

Q2. How do you say I am from England?

  • A) Vivo en Inglaterra
  • B) Soy de Inglaterra
  • C) Soy Inglaterra
  • D) Hablo inglés
▶ Answer & explanation

The question means: Choose the correct Spanish for "I am from England". ✅ Correct: B) Soy de Inglaterra

  • 🇬🇧 I am from England
  • 🇪🇸 Soy de Inglaterra — soy de = I am from. (Vivo en = I live in.)

Q3. What does Vivo en una ciudad mean?

  • A) I live in a village
  • B) I live in the countryside
  • C) I live in a city
  • D) I live on the coast
▶ Answer & explanation

The question means: Translate Vivo en una ciudad. ✅ Correct: C) I live in a city

  • 🇬🇧 I live in a city
  • 🇪🇸 una ciudad = a city. (un pueblo = a town/village, el campo = countryside, la costa = the coast)

Q4. Which means in the countryside?

▶ Answer & explanation

  • 🇬🇧 in the countryside
  • 🇪🇸 en el campo (la costa = coast, las montañas = mountains, un pueblo = a town)

Q5. Vivo en la costa. Where does this person live?

  • 🇬🇧 Scottish (boy)
  • 🇪🇸 escocés (boy, with accent) / escocesa (girl). (Escocia = Scotland.)

Q17. Choose the correct verb: ___ de Escocia pero ___ en Inglaterra.

  • A) Soy / vivo
  • B) Vivo / soy
  • C) Tengo / soy
  • D) Hablo / vivo
▶ Answer & explanation

The question means: Pick verbs for "I am from Scotland but I live in England." ✅ Correct: A) Soy / vivo

  • 🇬🇧 I am from Scotland but I live in England
  • 🇪🇸 Soy de Escocia pero vivo en Inglaterra — soy de = am from, vivo en = live in.

Q21. Which is the odd one out (not a language)?

  • A) el alemán
  • B) el chino
  • C) el árabe
  • D) la costa
▶ Answer & explanation

The question means: Three are languages; one is not. ✅ Correct: D) la costa

  • 🇬🇧 la costa = the coast (a place), not a language.
  • 🇪🇸 alemán = German, chino = Chinese, árabe = Arabic.
