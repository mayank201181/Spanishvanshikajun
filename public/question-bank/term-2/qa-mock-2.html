🟨 Term 2 — Question & Answer Mock Test 2

15 written questions. Write a full sentence first, then open the arrow.

Q1. Answer in Spanish: ¿De dónde eres? (say you are from Madrid, in the centre of Spain)

▶ Answer & explanation

The question means: "Where are you from?" — Madrid, centre of Spain. ✅ Model answer:

  • 🇪🇸 Soy de Madrid, en el centro de España.
  • 🇬🇧 I am from Madrid, in the centre of Spain.

Q2. Translate into Spanish: I live in the countryside.

▶ Answer & explanation

The question means: Put the sentence into Spanish. ✅ Model answer:

  • 🇪🇸 Vivo en el campo.
  • 🇬🇧 I live in the countryside.

Q3. Translate into Spanish: I am Irish and I live in the mountains. (girl)

▶ Answer & explanation

The question means: Put the sentence into Spanish. ✅ Model answer:

  • 🇪🇸 Soy irlandesa y vivo en las montañas.
  • 🇬🇧 I am Irish and I live in the mountains. (Girl → irlandesa.)

Q4. Translate into English: Soy alemán pero vivo en Escocia.

▶ Answer & explanation

The question means: Put the Spanish into English. ✅ Model answer:

  • 🇬🇧 I am German but I live in Scotland.
  • 🇪🇸 alemán = German (boy), pero = but.

Q5. Answer in Spanish: ¿Qué idiomas hablas? (say you speak English fluently and German quite well)

▶ Answer & explanation

The question means: "What languages do you speak?" — English fluently, German quite well. ✅ Model answer:

  • 🇪🇸 Hablo inglés con fluidez y alemán bastante bien.
  • 🇬🇧 I speak English fluently and German quite well.

Q6. Write the nationality (girl) for each country: España, Francia, Escocia.

▶ Answer & explanation

The question means: Give the feminine nationality for each country. ✅ Model answer:

  • 🇪🇸 española, francesa, escocesa.
  • 🇬🇧 Spanish, French, Scottish (all girl forms end in ‑a).

Q7. Translate into Spanish: Where do you live?

▶ Answer & explanation

The question means: Put the question into Spanish. ✅ Model answer:

  • 🇪🇸 ¿Dónde vives?
  • 🇬🇧 Where do you live?

Q8. Translate into Spanish: I live in a big and beautiful city.

▶ Answer & explanation

The question means: Put the sentence into Spanish (mind the endings). ✅ Model answer:

  • 🇪🇸 Vivo en una ciudad grande y bonita.
  • 🇬🇧 I live in a big and beautiful city. (ciudad feminine → bonita.)

Q9. Translate into English: Vengo de Gales pero ahora vivo en el este de España.

▶ Answer & explanation

The question means: Put the Spanish into English. ✅ Model answer:

  • 🇬🇧 I come from Wales but now I live in the east of Spain.
  • 🇪🇸 Vengo de = I come from, ahora = now, el este = the east.

Q10. Answer in Spanish: ¿Eres español/española? (say no, you are English — boy)

▶ Answer & explanation

The question means: "Are you Spanish?" — say no, you're English. ✅ Model answer:

  • 🇪🇸 No, soy inglés.
  • 🇬🇧 No, I am English. (A girl says inglesa.)

Q11. Translate into Spanish: I don't speak Chinese, but I want to speak Arabic.

▶ Answer & explanation

The question means: Put the sentence into Spanish. ✅ Model answer:

  • 🇪🇸 No hablo chino, pero quiero hablar árabe.
  • 🇬🇧 I don't speak Chinese, but I want to speak Arabic.

Q12. Translate into English: Mi ciudad está en el oeste de Inglaterra, cerca de Gales.

▶ Answer & explanation

The question means: Put the Spanish into English. ✅ Model answer:

  • 🇬🇧 My city is in the west of England, near Wales.
  • 🇪🇸 el oeste = the west, cerca de = near.

Q13. Answer in Spanish: ¿Vives cerca de la costa? (say yes, you live on the coast in the north)

▶ Answer & explanation

The question means: "Do you live near the coast?" — yes, on the coast in the north. ✅ Model answer:

  • 🇪🇸 Sí, vivo en la costa en el norte.
  • 🇬🇧 Yes, I live on the coast in the north.

Q14. Translate into Spanish: My friend is French and speaks French and Italian.

▶ Answer & explanation

The question means: Put the sentence into Spanish. ✅ Model answer:

  • 🇪🇸 Mi amigo es francés y habla francés e italiano.
  • 🇬🇧 My friend is French and speaks French and Italian. (Tip: y becomes e before an "i" sound → e italiano.)

Q15. Write 3 sentences in Spanish: where you're from, where you live, and the languages you speak and want to learn.

▶ Answer & explanation

The question means: Write a short Term‑2 paragraph. ✅ Model answer:

  • 🇪🇸 Soy de Irlanda. Vivo en un pueblo en la costa. Hablo inglés con fluidez y quiero hablar español bastante bien.
  • 🇬🇧 I am from Ireland. I live in a town on the coast. I speak English fluently and I want to speak Spanish quite well.
